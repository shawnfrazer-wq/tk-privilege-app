# TK Privilege: decisions from the final review, 21 September 2026

These sit alongside rules.md. Where they disagree, rules.md is the later document and wins.

## 1. Booking requests go by email

The app does not write a booking or a "requested" appointment into the diary. When she taps Request this booking:

1. The app saves the request through a CRM function (salon.privilege_request_booking: service, stylist, the day and time she chose, a note).
2. The CRM emails info@tatianakarelina.co.uk from the salon's Google mailbox. Subject: "Booking request: [name], [service] with [stylist], [day date] at [time]". Body: her name, mobile, the service, the stylist, the day and time she chose, and a link to her client card in the CRM.
3. The desk books it in the diary as normal and lets her know.
4. The app shows it as Requested until an appointment for her appears in the diary on or after the request date. The request then closes on its own and the app shows it as Your Next Appointment. If nothing is booked within 7 days, the request closes and the app stops showing it.

The time is not held while she waits. The Book screen shows her stylist's free times, read from the diary through a CRM function.

A booking made in the app never earns the 100 point booking bonus.

## 2. Review checks

The app records when she taps Google or Trustpilot (salon.privilege_review_tap). The CRM shows a Review claims list. The desk presses Pay 250. The CRM enforces 250 per platform, once each in any rolling 6 months.

## 3. Tiers

Silver 1 point per £1, Gold 1.25, Black 1.5, all with the Care Card. Same bands for every tier. No other tier benefits.

## 4. Prices

All prices in the CRM are rounded to the nearest £5. Rewards show the CRM price as it is.

## 5. Holiday

No Holiday screen. Holidays are at the salon's discretion.

## 6. Spending points

Points are earned only at care visits and can be spent on anything, at any visit, including new sets and pieces. Never on deposits or gift vouchers.
